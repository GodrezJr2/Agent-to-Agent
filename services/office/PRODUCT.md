# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One technical owner, sometimes a small team, running a self-hosted AI workspace. They open the office to hand work to a team of agents, then keep it open beside their editor to watch progress, read results and step in. They use it both at night in a dim room while coding and during the day on a bright screen, so it must work equally well in light and dark themes.

## Product Purpose

A2A Office runs a team of AI agents that are real A2A v1 servers and think through the owner's 9router gateway. The owner hires agents into an org chart, messages the office, and watches delegation, tool use and results happen live, with a pixel-art office as the living view of who is doing what. Success: the owner trusts what the team is doing at a glance, can find any result or file quickly, and never has to leave the office to check which models or providers are available.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, alive. It is a professional tool in the lineage of Linear and Raycast: quiet surfaces, crisp type, fast interactions, dense where it matters. The pixel office supplies the warmth and life; the chrome around it stays composed and never competes with it.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: uniform rounded cards, purple or blue gradients, emoji icons, hero-metric tiles.
- Toy UI: loud saturated colors, childish shapes, chrome that imitates the game art.
- Hacker terminals: green-on-black, neon glows, everything monospace.

## Design Principles

1. The office is the window, the chrome is the frame. Pixel art is the only playful surface; everything around it is restrained so the scene reads.
2. State before decoration. Color, motion and emphasis only ever signal something real: an agent working, a task failing, the gateway down.
3. Show provenance. Every reply shows who produced it, through which delegation and which model, so the owner can trust or question it.
4. No dead ends. Empty states and errors teach the next action (hire a lead, pick a model, fix the gateway key).
5. Dense but legible. Operator density is welcome as long as hierarchy stays obvious at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes. Full keyboard operation for chat, mentions, tabs and editor tools. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (no pulsing or walking emphasis beyond the canvas itself). Never encode state by color alone; pair it with text or shape.
